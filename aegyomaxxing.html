SION RIKU YUSHI JAEHEE RYO SAKUYA

Jaehee (Korean: 재희; Japanese: ジェヒ), formerly known as Daeyoung (대영), is a South Korean singer under SM Entertainment. He is a member of the boy group NCT and its sub-unit NCT WISH.

Why Jaehee is my favorite: His voice is great. That's something you don't see very often in the K-Pop industry.

Riku (Korean: 리쿠; Japanese: リク) is a Japanese rapper and singer under SM Entertainment. He is a member of the boy group NCT and its sub-unit NCT WISH.

Why Riku is my favorite: He is very funny. He's a very silly guy.
